Built for teams that can't afford downtime

Watchtower was created because every second of downtime costs money, trust, and users. We built the monitoring tool we wished existed.

Our Mission

Give every engineering team the confidence that their services are running. Not dashboards that look good--tools that catch problems before users notice.

How It Works

Watchtower runs checks from multiple global regions every 10 to 300 seconds. When something breaks, you get an alert with root cause analysis--not just "it's down".

Security First

All data encrypted in transit and at rest. SOC 2 compliant infrastructure. Your monitoring data never leaves our secure environment.

Built by Engineers

We've been on-call at 3 AM. We've debugged cascading failures. Watchtower is the tool we built to make those nights less painful.
